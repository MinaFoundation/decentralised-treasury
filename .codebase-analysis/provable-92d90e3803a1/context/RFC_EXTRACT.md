The RFC describes a fully on-chain, community-controlled, upgradable Mina treasury. It proposes provable proposal handling, voting, execution, and accounting; token-weighted voting based on historical staking-ledger snapshots; delegated voting with self-override; nullifier-based double-count prevention; configurable quorum and majority; proposal, exploration, voting, cooldown, and post-cooldown stages; proposer bonds; break-glass pause; progressive fund migration; and permissionless/self-hostable infrastructure.

Important maturity qualifications: treasury upgrades are optional; voting delegation, upgradability, multiple delegations, and some safety/performance features are later-stage; quorum and passing criteria remain subject to research; break-glass details and sunset are not final; community comments dispute quorum feasibility, delegated voting concentration, bonds, and milestone payments. Absence of these optional/future items is not automatically an implementation defect.
